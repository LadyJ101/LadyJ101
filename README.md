> *"You cannot fit a whole wardrobe into a tiny backpack without rolling everything super tight. We run into the exact same wall when trying to put heavy deep learning models onto small hardware like microchips and edge devices."*

I am a **Computer Engineering undergraduate** operating at the intersection of **silicon physics, hardware design, and edge intelligence**. My work bridges the gap between low-level RTL logic and high-performance software—designing compact neural networks, hardware co-processors, and intelligent systems that survive real-world physical constraints.
